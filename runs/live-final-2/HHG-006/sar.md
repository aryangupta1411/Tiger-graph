This report concerns internal case AC-HHG-006, filed for an undocumented coordinated fraud pattern identified as an under-threshold burst. The subject is customer C07297, cardholder of debit card C07297-K1 (mastercard network), whose card was used through two device profiles: "Trident/7.0 | Windows 7 | ie 11.0 for desktop | 1920x1080" and "iOS Device | iOS 11.2.1 | mobile safari 11.0 | 2208x1242"; no other cards were found connected to these device profiles at the time of review. On 2016-11-21 at 20:00 an online purchase of $478.95 (transaction 3476602, product code C, billing region 264.0) was made on the Trident/IE device profile, newly seen on the account. At 20:10 an online purchase of $456.96 (transaction 3476633, billing region 476.0) followed on the iOS device profile, also newly seen, and at 20:24 a further online purchase of $488.04 (transaction 3476665, billing region 476.0) occurred on the same iOS device profile. At 20:30 the flagged transaction, an online purchase of $482.12 (transaction 3476682, billing region 264.0) on the Trident/IE device profile, completed the sequence; the customer stated they never made this $482.12 purchase. All four transactions were placed within 30 minutes, all online, all under product code C, and all priced between $450 and $499.99, just under a likely $500 authorization threshold; the graph shows the identical amount-and-timing shape on 12 other cards within the same 30-day window and on five closed cases previously confirmed as fraud of this type. This activity is inconsistent with the cardholder's baseline as of the case opening: in the 199 prior transactions on this card, the median amount was $62.93 and the maximum was $1,104.00, the card had recorded only 4 online transactions in the preceding 30 days, and neither device profile had more than 3 prior uses on the card, with first use of the Trident/IE profile on 2016-07-12 and the iOS profile on 2016-07-26. No prior suspicious activity report has been filed on this card, and no prior report exists for the two device profiles or any connected cards; OFAC screening of the customer and card identifiers against the SDN list returned no match. A case (AC-HHG-006) has been opened and the matter escalated to an analyst for review of the wider coordinated pattern; a block of card C07297-K1 is recommended and awaits team-lead approval, and the filing of this report is recommended and awaits fraud-manager approval, covering total suspicious activity of $1,906.07 across the four transactions described.
