This report documents an undocumented coordinated fraud pattern identified in internal case AC-HHG-014. The subject is customer C13487, whose debit card C13487-K1 shows two online transactions on a device profile, `SM-G935F Build/NRD90M | Android 7.0 | chrome 62.0 for android | 1920x1080`, that is shared with 27 other connected cards in the same wave, including cards C06031-K2, C10326-K1, C04274-K1, C10193-K2, C11670-K2, C09049-K1, C01289-K1, C05448-K2, C12796-K1, C09906-K1, C01996-K2, C11082-K1, C08168-K1, C06710-K1, C13291-K1, C02975-K1, C02910-K1, C04108-K2, C09975-K1 and C03676-K2, among others. On 2016-11-15 at 20:30, card C13487-K1 made a $112.37 online purchase on this device profile, marked as a new device for the account. On 2016-11-22 at 16:11, the same card made a second online purchase of $74.96 (product code C, billing region 191.0) on the identical device profile, again flagged as a new device; this second transaction is what triggered the analyst's request for review after several cards that month were observed using the same unusual device profile. The device profile is routed through an anonymous proxy address and, as of the case opening, was associated with 28 cards active in the current wave, 19 of which had already transacted on the profile before this case was opened, indicating coordinated, non-organic use across many customer accounts rather than a single cardholder's device history. This pattern is unusual against the cardholder's own baseline as of opening: across 71 prior transactions dating back to 2016-07-04, the card's median amount was $55.92 and maximum was $225.94, with regular activity split between billing regions 191.0 and 272.0 and only one other device on file (a Windows/edge profile first seen 2016-10-02); the shared, proxy-routed device profile had no prior history on this card before 2016-11-15. No prior suspicious activity report has been filed on this card; the same device profile or connected cards were the subject of four closed cases, CC-2649, CC-3035, CC-2971 and CC-2985, all confirmed fraud with reports filed between August and September 2016. OFAC screening of the customer and card identifiers against the SDN list returned no match. In response to this activity, the agent opened case AC-HHG-014 and placed the connected cards sharing this device profile under monitoring; a block of card C13487-K1 is recommended and awaits team-lead approval, this filing is recommended and awaits fraud-manager approval, and the matter has been escalated to an analyst for further review of the coordinated pattern; total suspicious activity on this card identified to date is $187.33.
